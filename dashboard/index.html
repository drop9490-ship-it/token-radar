How the radar scores a protocol

base value = TVL × sector multiple
growth-adjusted = base × growth
implied value = base × growth × maturity × chains × risk
implied FDV = TVL × sector FDV multiple × growth × maturity × chains × risk

Sector multiple — fitted, not assumed
Each category's multiple is fitted to the snapshot's own live-token protocols, not taken from a table. The calibration runs on one live protocol per token family (latest deployment per family, the same rule the board uses), so no family prices its own sector twice; on this snapshot the 128 live protocols already are one per family, so the dedup removes none. The fit is a log-log regression of market cap on TVL — log(mcap) = a + b·log(TVL) — run on the category's live protocols with market caps winsorised at the 90th percentile (2.975×). The fitted curve is blended in log space with the category's static median (the radar-4 figure: winsorised, shrunk toward the global median with weight n / (n + 4), clamped to 0.02–1.50) at weight n / (n + 4). A category with fewer than 5 live protocols takes the global curve (slope 0.563, R² 0.268); a category with none takes the global median. The blended multiple is clamped to 0.02–1.50, so a steep curve cannot extrapolate a multiple past any multiple the market shows for a whole sector. The multiple is a property of the snapshot, never of a name. The fitted table below shows every category's slope, its multiple at $100M and $1B of TVL, and the blind-test errors that decided whether it uses the curve or the median — and the detail view shows the comps themselves.
Implied FDV
The snapshot carries no FDV and no supply data, so no per-category FDV multiple can be observed from it. The FDV column applies the entry's own uniform listing assumption instead: FDV = market cap × 12.5, i.e. 8% of supply circulating at listing, the figure ICO pricing guides cite (Optimism's observed FDV / market cap is 12.9×). The sector FDV / TVL multiple is the fitted sector multiple × 12.5 — the curve is fitted on market cap, and the FDV column is that same curve shifted by log 12.5. It is a comparison bound, printed here and never hidden — and it never moves a rank: the board ranks on implied market cap alone.
Blind validation — only what passes is used
Every term in the model is tested on data it never saw. Leave-one-out: each of the 128 live protocols is held out in turn and predicted from the rest; the selected model misses by 1.751 in mean absolute log error, against 1.876 for the plain median and 1.815 for using the curve everywhere. Walk-forward: fit on protocols listed before 1 Jan 2024 and test on the 51 listed after (1.799 vs 1.853 for the median); fit before 1 Jan 2025 and test on the 31 after (1.858 vs 1.906). The method each category uses is chosen by the combined blind evidence — its leave-one-out error plus its walk-forward error, both splits pooled — the lower combined error wins: 26 of 34 categories use the curve. The 8 that stay on the median are CEX, Canonical Bridge, Derivatives, Lending, Liquid Restaking, Payments, Privacy and Yield. Privacy is the one category the walk-forward flips: its curve wins leave-one-out (1.750 vs 1.791) but loses walk-forward (1.729 vs 1.557), so the combined evidence keeps it on the median. The walk-forward numbers are the errors of the method the combined evidence chose, not a held-out check of it. The table below shows both errors per category, so a reader can see exactly where the curve earned its place and where it did not.
Net value — farm, thin or skip
Every farm plan is netted to the dollar, at the conservative end. Expected value is 20% × min($3,000, 0.01% of the protocol's LOW-END implied FDV) — the 5th percentile of its implied FDV across the same 200 bootstrap refits that give the rank ranges — so a row whose value the calibration cannot pin down is netted on what it is worth at the bottom of its range, not on its point estimate. Only about 20% of airdrops pay meaningfully, a typical wallet's allocation runs $1,000–$3,000 (Arbitrum's typical; Hyperliquid paid tens of thousands), and 0.01% is the entry's own wallet-share assumption, stated here. All-in cost is the chain's gas and bridge for 12 weeks plus 12 hours of time at $25 an hour — about $304–$308, and the time is the biggest part. Net = expected value − all-in. Farm when net is +$100 or better, Skip when it is −$100 or worse, Thin in between. The break-even allocation is the all-in cost itself, and the break-even FDV — the low-end FDV that would make the farm break even — is all-in × 50,000. The detail view shows the low-end FDV, the expected value, the all-in, the net and the verdict for the row — 136 rows are Farm, 37 Thin, 35 Skip.
Rank ranges — 200 refits
The board's order is a point estimate, and the middle of the board is dense: neighbouring ranks swap on small calibration moves. Each row carries its rank range across 200 refits — the 128 live comps resampled with replacement, the curves and medians re-fitted, the board re-scored and re-ranked, the 5th–95th percentile rank kept. The refits are seeded, so they reproduce exactly. The top of the board is stable (rank 1 sits at #1–#1, rank 2 at #2–#2); the median row's range spans 66 places, and 128 of 208 rows span more than 50. Read neighbouring ranks as a band, not a ladder. The same refits give each row's low-end implied FDV — the 5th percentile of its refit FDVs — which is what the netting above uses, and the detail view shows it beside the point estimate.
Growth — the radar's own formula, printed in full
The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −40%…+60%. Gains: growth = 1 + 0.30 × √(growth / 60%), so a +60% week is worth +30%. Losses: growth = 1 + 0.50 × (growth / 100), so a −40% week costs −20%. No published standard growth-adjusted market-cap / TVL formula exists, so this adjustment is the radar's own, stated here rather than hidden — and the detail view shows the base value beside the growth-adjusted one, so a reader can see exactly what the week's move did.
Maturity
0.55 + 0.45 × min(age, 1095 days) / 1095. A protocol tracked under 90 days takes a 0.85 youth discount; one with no listing date is scored at 365 days.
Chains
1 + 0.03 × ln(chains, capped at ln 10): a protocol live on more chains earns a small diversification premium.
Risk
0.90 when the protocol's TVL is under $10M, else 1.00. A thin pool can halve on a single withdrawal, so the radar discounts it. The threshold is twice the snapshot's $5M floor. This is the model's only risk leg: the snapshot carries no audit field, so no audit claim is made anywhere on this page.

What counts as pre-token

A protocol records no token when its DefiLlama record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol. The board keeps every protocol on the snapshot's watchlist and adds every other pre-token protocol below the floor that is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades and not an exchange product. A name ending in a release (V2, V3, 2.0) is a deployment, not a protocol: the board keeps a versioned protocol once, at its latest deployment — Aera V3, not V2 beside it — and a versioned row whose family has no other deployment in the snapshot is kept as it stands. The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: 168 of this board's 208 rows sit below it. A protocol is said to record no token, never to have none — its record may simply not carry one.

How to read a farm plan

Each plan is eight steps over 12 weeks, derived from the protocol's own record — its category, its chains and its age — with a cost and a time on every step. Capital follows the chain's tier: Ethereum $1,000 and up, Arbitrum and Optimism $100–$500, Base $50–$300, Solana $50–$500. Gas is the October 2026 guide's per-chain figure (Ethereum $0.25–$0.39 a swap, Arbitrum $0.01–$0.05, Base $0.003–$0.03, Optimism $0.02–$0.05, Solana $0.00025); bridging from mainnet to a rollup costs $2–$10, and withdrawing from one back takes 7 days. Every plan prices its time — 1 hour a week × 12 weeks = 12 hours, about $300 at $25 an hour — because gas is the smallest cost and time is the biggest. Every plan states the odds: only about 20% of airdrops pay meaningfully, most pay under $100 a wallet, many tokens fall 70–90% in the first month, and sybil filters disqualify about 40% of claimants. Step 5 of every plan does the netting at the low end: it prints the expected value from the low-end implied FDV, the all-in cost, the net and the break-even, and the verdict — Farm, Thin or Skip — so a plan that costs more than it can pay is labelled Skip on the row itself. No step quotes a yield the snapshot does not carry: the rate a protocol pays is read from its own rewards page, never invented here. Never pay to claim: a real claim costs nothing but gas, and only the link the protocol itself publishes is the claim.

Why this model

The question is what a token would be worth if a protocol launched one, and the only market evidence for that is what the market already pays for the same kind of protocol: the median market-cap / TVL of the live-token protocols in its category — one per token family, so no family prices its own sector twice. A median, not a mean, so one frothy token cannot set a sector. Ratios are winsorised at the 90th percentile (2.975× here) for the same reason, and a category's median is shrunk toward the global median with weight n / (n + 4), so a category with one live protocol — 12 of the 34 here rest on a single comp — cannot price itself off one data point. The FDV column runs the same evidence through the entry's stated 12.5× listing assumption, so the two columns disagree exactly where supply still has to unlock. The size term itself is fitted, not assumed: a log-log curve of market cap on TVL per sector, blended with the median, and the blind test — not the builder — decides per sector which one the board uses. Growth is dampened because a single hot week is mostly noise, while a falling week is information — and the adjustment is printed, with the base beside the adjusted figure, so it can be checked. Maturity rewards survival: a protocol that has run for years with no token has earned the right to be scored at full weight, and a protocol under 90 days is discounted because it has not. Thin TVL is discounted because it can vanish. Nothing in the model names a protocol: the same five factors score every row, and the board is the whole rankable set the snapshot holds.

Worked example — rank 1, Steakhouse Financial

Steakhouse Financial (Risk Curators) holds $2,753,981,495 of TVL, tracked for 543 days on 11 chains, its TVL down 2.2% in the last 7 days. Risk Curators rests on a single live protocol, so its size curve is the global curve (slope 0.563, intercept 6.106): at this TVL the curve alone would price it at 0.033×, the sector median is 0.766×, and the blend — 20% curve, 80% median, because n = 1 — gives a sector multiple of 0.409×. Its base value is $1,127,685,764; its sector FDV multiple is 5.118× (the sector multiple × 12.5 at full precision), so its base FDV is $14,096,072,048. Growth is ×0.989, so the growth-adjusted value is $1,115,317,870. Maturity ×0.773, chains ×1.069 and risk ×1.000 give an implied value of $921,874,955 and an implied FDV of $11,523,436,940 — the board's rank 1, and its rank across 200 refits is #1–#1. Its net value is +$293: the expected value is about $600 — 20% × $3,000, the cap, because even its low-end implied FDV of $4,853,162,097 (5th percentile of 200 refits, against a point FDV of $11,523,436,940) puts 0.01% of it above the cap — minus $307 all-in, so its farm plan reads Farm. Factors are shown rounded to three decimals; the board uses the full-precision factors, so recomputing from the printed figures agrees within about 0.2%.

Methodology: every figure on this page is computed from the sealed DefiLlama snapshot dated 14 Nov 2026 — TVL, 7-day change, age and chains per protocol, and the sector multiples from the snapshot's own 128 live-token protocols. Nothing is fetched live, and no protocol name is special-cased.

Not financial advice. Implied values are this entry's own claim, never a prediction that a token will be issued or what it will be worth. A protocol that records no token may still have one its record does not carry.
