How the radar scores a protocol

implied value = TVL × sector multiple × growth × maturity × chains

Sector multiple
Each category's median market-cap / TVL among the snapshot's own live-token protocols, ratios winsorised at the 90th percentile, then shrunk toward the global median with weight n / (n + 4) and clamped to 0.02–1.50. A category with no live protocol takes the global median. The multiple is a property of the snapshot, never of a name.
Growth
The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −40%…+60%. Gains are dampened with a square root (a +60% week is worth +30%); losses count in full (a −40% week costs −20%).
Maturity
0.55 + 0.45 × min(age, 1095 days) / 1095. A protocol tracked under 90 days takes a 0.85 youth discount; one with no listing date is scored at 365 days.
Chains
1 + 0.03 × ln(chains, capped at ln 10): a protocol live on more chains earns a small diversification premium.

What counts as pre-token

A protocol records no token when its DefiLlama record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol. The board keeps every protocol on the snapshot's watchlist and adds every other pre-token protocol below the floor that is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, not an exchange product and not a versioned pool. The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: 158 of this board's 198 rows sit below it. A protocol is said to record no token, never to have none — its record may simply not carry one.

How to read a farm plan

Each plan is seven steps derived from the protocol's own record — its category, its chains, its TVL and its age — with a cost and a time on every step. Capital is sized to the protocol: $50 under $100M TVL, $100 under $1B, $150 under $5B, $250 above. Hold windows run 30 to 60 days by age. Never pay to claim: a real claim costs nothing but gas, and only the link the protocol itself publishes is the claim.
